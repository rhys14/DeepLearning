    Every model, one galaxy.

    Upload an image and every model classifies it into one of the ten Galaxy10 DECaLS classes. Their full confidence distributions appear below.

      Confidence distributions

      Each column is one model's probabilities across all ten classes, adding to 100%. The solid bar is that model's prediction.

      Every upload is center-cropped to a square and resized to 256×256 first, so every model sees the same galaxy. Then each model applies the preprocessing it was trained with: the baseline CNN and DINOv2 take raw 256×256 pixels, VGG16 and ResNet-18 take a 224×224 center crop with ImageNet normalization, and GalaxyDETR takes the whole 256×256 image with ImageNet normalization. GalaxyDETR's answer comes from its most confident object query, and its distribution covers the ten classes with the "no galaxy" slot taken out.
